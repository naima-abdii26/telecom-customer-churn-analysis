# Telecom Customer Churn & Subscription Optimization

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 1,000 telecommunications subscriptions to identify customer churn patterns and understand which subscription plans and customer segments have the highest churn rates.

## Business Objectives
- Measure the overall customer churn rate.
- Identify subscription plans with high churn rates.
- Compare churn across service types, customer locations, and age groups.
- Identify monthly charges associated with churned subscriptions.
- Provide evidence-based recommendations for further investigation.

## Tools & Skills
- **MySQL:** Data profiling, data cleaning, joins, aggregation, and business analysis.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Power Query, data quality checks, PivotTables, and churn-rate analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development — **In Progress**.

## Key Findings
- **Overall churn:** 236 of 1,000 subscriptions were churned (23.60%).
- **Highest plan churn rate:** Mobile Data 5GB at 37.89%.
- **Highest service-type churn rate:** Data Only at 32.11%.
- **Highest location churn rate:** Bosaso at 30.82%.
- **Monthly charges associated with churn:** $8,196.53 across all churned subscriptions.
- **Highest plan-level monthly charges associated with churn:** Ultimate Connect at $2,229.52.

These findings describe the observed dataset. Monthly charges associated with churn should not be interpreted as guaranteed future revenue losses, and the analysis does not establish the causes of churn.
